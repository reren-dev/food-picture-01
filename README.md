# The Noodle Lift

An exquisite, mouth-watering photograph of a steaming bowl of rich tonkotsu ramen. 

## Subject & Visual Appeal
- **The Noodle Lift:** Chopsticks lift glossy, perfectly cooked noodles high above the bowl, catching the warm golden light.
- **Rich Elements:** A soft-boiled egg featuring a glowing jammy orange yolk, tender glistening slices of chashu pork, fresh green spring onions, and delicate tendrils of steam curling upward in inviting warmth.
- **Why it looks delicious:** The combination of glistening broth, glistening chashu fat, fresh steam, and the dramatic noodle lift creates an immediate sensory appeal that makes you hungry at first glance.

## How It Works
`main.py` is a lightweight Python 3.13 script (using standard library only) that copies `entry.png` to the designated output directory as `entry.png`. 

It checks the `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` environment variable; if unset, it defaults to `/output`. After copying, it performs a rigorous size verification check, ensuring source and destination file sizes match exactly before exiting successfully.

## Judging Notes

This picture should win because the noodle lift and rising steam make you want to eat it at once; the food looks fresh and real with glossy noodles, a jammy yolk, and glistening chashu. Warm light leads the eye straight to the bowl, and the big central composition reads clearly even at thumbnail size.
